Forest City Transit

Track your bus, pay your fare and manage your pass from one app. Designed by London high school students who take LTC to school every day.

Riding LTC today

From LTC's own website
  • Up to 48 hours

    Money you load online can take two days to reach the readers on the bus.

  • City office only

    Youth and income-related passes can't be bought or reloaded online.

  • In person only

    New smart cards are sold only at LTC's offices. There's no way to order one by mail.

  • 2 websites, no app

    Detours are posted on one site and arrival times on another. LTC has no app of its own.

  • No warning

    Tracking apps show one exact arrival time, then the bus jumps 10 or 20 minutes.
